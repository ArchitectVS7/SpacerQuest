The scoping measurement this is compared against — 80 fuel, a DC-12 nav check, 53.8cr per
attempt, 101 of 120 seeds richer without the verb — is `TASKS.md:18-24`.

**The caveat the headline rests on, stated with the headline** (§9.8 caveat 1): **the two arms
are not rng-paired.** Removing actions shifts the within-day event-index forks, so arm B is a
different sample rather than a fixed control — which is why the verdict is carried by the
**paired sign count** (85/120) and never by a mean difference. Arm B's median falling slightly
(90,135 → 88,107) is drift, not signal.

**The credit column understates the rebuilt verb, and it still does not rescue it** (§9.4).
Gross salvage fell 9,688 → 1,704 per run *by design*: 25% of boards are an authored dead end,
42% of the weight defers its payout to a recovery dusk, and most of the per-board value is now
items, questline hooks, NPC standing and fragments — which `finalCredits` cannot see. 153
unique items and 440 POI fragments over 120 runs are worth something to a career and exactly
zero to the column the question was asked in. The sign count is measured on credits anyway,
because credits are what the question asked about.

**The honest reading available to the owner** (a judgement, stated as one): the ladder's top

look at after pricing. A ladder that costs something to hold is *supposed* to cost something;
whether it should cost everything at the top is the question.

42.5% of boards, not the predicted 14.1% (the shipped weights sum to 42 on `recoveryDays > 0`,

**Re-pricing is R-series and an owner call.** `EXPLORATION_FUEL_COST = 80` and

### 3.2 The verdict — with the inertness bound stated FIRST

**The bound, before the lift, exactly as §10.0 orders it.** Disposition reaches the interceptor
draw only **~25%** of the time, and on **~70%** of the draws it does reach, it is exactly inert.
`selectEncounterInterceptor` enters the named pool only on `rng.next() < 0.25`
(`packages/engine/src/actions/travel.ts:389` — verified at HEAD), and 24.70% of the fleet's
23,100 player interceptions were named. Of those 5,706 named draws, **3,969 (69.56%) saw a pool
in which every candidate sat at disposition 0** — a draw on which `chooseWeighted` is
byte-identical to the old uniform pick, by its own doc comment.

**Now the lift.** The share of NAMED interceptions flown by a captain the player had wronged
went **4.22% → 29.28%** on the `gambler` arm, against an analytic uniform counterfactual over
the same reconstructed pools of **1.78% → 9.90%** — a 2.37× → 2.96× lift. Fleet-wide the same
number is 5.87% → 10.13% against 2.76% → 4.22%.

**So the reach change did not make disposition matter more OFTEN; it made it matter more
SHARPLY where it already could.** The gambler's inertness rate is what carries that: **76.26% →
31.65%**, and the chosen captain's mean disposition (−1.378) is 4.7× more hostile than their
pool's (−0.294).

**The control, and it is exact.** `fighter`, `explorer`, `veteran` and `greedy` never open a
Hangout, and **every** M5 figure for them is byte-identical across the two arms. That is the
evidence the probe was sound: the movement is the Hangout and nothing else. The capstone's own
`balance:diff` reports the same moved/unchanged split from an entirely independent instrument
(§3.5).

with the cast that the player *chose*.

**A reachable Hangout is one the player actually walks into:** the gambler reaches 13.87 of the
14 authored ports in a 120-day career. The reach change is exercised almost exhaustively, not
theoretical headroom. And the tables stopped being free money in the way a 10-seed sample first
suggested: `expectedValuePerDare` fell 159.56 → 120.14 (still firmly positive) once the law of
large numbers reached a verb that could previously be played 27 times in a career.

### 3.4 The counter-evidence — reported because it cuts against the track

**`BondIntervention` fired 42 → 34 times**, and captains reaching their own `bondHook.activateAt`
went 583 → 553. The bond hook is a `disposition >= activateAt` gate, and `dare` applies
`DARE_WIN_DISPOSITION = −2` when the player **wins** — the player won 8,859 of 15,461 hands
(57.3%), so **the modal outcome of a night at the tables is a slightly angrier dealer.** The
reach change grew the grudge tail (20 → 143) and left the friendship tail almost where it was
(42 → 50).

**The reach change therefore pushed standing AWAY from the one hook that rewards it.** This is
the strongest single argument for **F-101-4** (surface `befriend` / `meet` / `insult`): the only
verbs that can move standing upward are the three with no UI. It is not a defect in the shipped
arithmetic — it falls directly out of it — but it is a design consequence the owner should see.

**`fighter` is worth one sentence.** It has the highest wronged share of any policy (35.14%)
and the lowest inertness (51.03%), and it got there entirely through violence, and is unmoved
by this track. **That is the shape of the problem §1.5 named: before the Hangout was reachable,
the only way to make the interceptor draw mean anything was to shoot people.**

## §5 · THE NPC HANGOUT FAUCET

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **`executeSocialize` pays NPCs 150 credits out of nothing, takes 50 back into nothing, and
> never asks whether the port it names has a bar.** It has no counterparty and no `hasHangout`
> gate. Both were easy to leave open when the player-facing verb existed at **1 of 28 systems**.
> The player's Hangout is now a real, authored **14-port** system with six axes per port — and
> the cast is minting credits at a bar the player is explicitly told does not exist, at
> **37.96%** of the places the verb fires. **This is a decision at this gate. It is D3.**

### 5.1 The mechanism, verified at HEAD

- `executeSocialize` (`packages/engine/src/npc.ts:1824`, called from `resolveNpcDay` at
  `:1950`) adds `NPC_SOCIALIZE_WIN_CREDITS = 150` on a success and subtracts
  `NPC_SOCIALIZE_LOSS_CREDITS = 50` on a failure (`packages/content/src/ideals.ts:73`, `:76`)
  — **against no counterparty at all.** A net **+4.86cr per captain-day minted into the field**.
- The player's Dare, by contrast, is a **zero-sum transfer**: `creditsDelta` is added to the
  player and subtracted from the dealer's purse off the same `wager`
  (`packages/engine/src/actions/hangout.ts:285-290`).
- **`executeSocialize` never reads `hasHangout`.** Verified: `grep -n "hasHangout"
  packages/engine/src/npc.ts` returns **no match**. That absence is the defect.
- The 150cr ante is an **inline `+ 50` literal** on a module-private engine constant —
  `npc.ts:1831` reads `npc.credits < NPC_BROKE_CREDITS + 50`, with `NPC_BROKE_CREDITS = 100`
  at `npc.ts:471`. A content instance living in the engine, which is the exact class of thing
  this track exists to separate.

### 5.2 Every deferral reason, re-argued on the measured numbers

**§5.1's reason 3 was the load-bearing one, and it has been measured away.** The argument was:
*"the faucet is what keeps dealer purses solvent, and the dealer's purse is the binding cap on
the player's wager."* §10.5 measured which of the three clamp terms actually binds over 15,461
dares: **the BAND binds 88.93%, the DEALER's purse only 10.97%, and the player's own credits
bound ZERO of 15,461.** Closing the faucet would shrink realisable stakes on roughly one hand
in nine, not on the modal hand. **The argument that carried the deferral no longer carries it.**
(F-101-1 tells the same story from the other side: the N-series made the cast rich enough that
the dealer-purse cap is a solved problem, and the **band** is now the live constraint.)

**§5.2's magnitude fell by a factor of 2.5.** The off-Hangout share went **95.91% → 37.96%**
(§10.6) — below the ~50% §5.2 predicted, because the cast concentrates on the core lane, which
is exactly where the bars went. So §5.2's reason 3 is now a claim about deleting **~38%** of
the verb's occurrences rather than ~96%. It is a weaker claim. **It still owes a capstone.**

**The fiction consequence, which the spec itself calls the strongest argument T-130 will have.**
`executeSocialize`'s prose reads *"cleaned up at the {system} Hangout tables"* (`npc.ts:1845`)
and *"bought a round at the {system} Hangout"* (`:1851`). Those feed `lastAction.details`
(written once per captain per dusk at `npc.ts:2005`), which `hangoutRumors` embeds **verbatim**
into the rumour table (`packages/engine/src/actions/hangout.ts:81`). Measured: **139,815
captain-days per 960 runs of rumour-mill prose naming a Hangout at a port where the player is
explicitly told there is none** — at 14 ports instead of 1, and surfaced in the pane.

**§5.3's ante still gates only the cast.** The player's Dare clamps to their own credits, so a
broke captain at the keyboard is never locked out. But the 150cr ante locks out exactly the
destitute captains a night at the tables would help, sending them to `brokeIdle`.

### 5.3 The ask, as three named options

| option | what it touches | what it costs | what it buys |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **(a) Close the faucet** — make the NPC verb zero-sum, or delete the credit movement | `packages/engine/src/npc.ts`, `packages/content/src/ideals.ts` | No save change. **Owes a capstone.** Shrinks dealer purses, now bounded at ~11% of stakes rather than the modal hand | Cast-economy parity: the player's Dare and the cast's Socialize stop being different economies |
| **(b) Gate on `hasHangout`** | One `STAR_SYSTEMS` read in `executeSocialize` | Deletes ~38% of the verb's occurrences, so it **moves the cast's verb mix** and **owes a capstone** | Closes the rumour-mill inconsistency at all 14 ports; the fiction stops contradicting the map |
| **(c) Defer again** | Nothing | Nothing now | **If this is the choice, record WHICH argument survives** — reason 3 does not, on the measured 10.97%, and reason 2's "a number this track is about to change" has now been changed and measured |

Options (a) and (b) are independent; either can be taken without the other, and both are
inside `docs/NPC_REDESIGN.md:229`'s vacated row, i.e. inside **D2**.

**No recommendation is offered on D3.** It is a cast-economy ruling the owner explicitly
reserved, and the point of this section is that the evidence has moved, not that the answer has.
